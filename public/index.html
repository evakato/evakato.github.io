Hello! I’m Eva.

Professionally, I’m a research engineer at the deep-tech startup Braid. I co-lead our geometry team, developing robust geometry processing software for our engine that automatically generates manufacturable engineering parts.

I graduated from Purdue University with a BSc in Computer Science, where I worked on VR research advised by Prof. Voicu Popescu. I then did an MSc in Computer Science at Utrecht University, working on vector graphics research with Prof. Jiří Kosinka and Dr. Tim Ophelders.
